# Manual Quality Checklist

## Layout And Navigation

- Confirm the sticky navigation remains accessible on a mobile-width viewport.
- Confirm each primary navigation link lands on the expected anchored section.
- Confirm a deep link such as `/#work-with-us` opens the correct section.

## Content Structure

- Confirm the locked hero headline and subhead match the current PRD.
- Confirm official outbound links open in a new browser tab.
- Confirm placeholder copy is clearly marked where PRD content is still unresolved.

## Adaptive Form

- Confirm the form defaults to the `work_with_us` path.
- Confirm each selector path changes the visible fields and submit label.
- Confirm required-field validation appears when the form is submitted incomplete.
- Confirm the development success message appears after a valid local submission.

## Responsiveness

- Confirm the layout works cleanly at 390px, 768px, and 1280px widths.
- Confirm all form controls remain usable with touch interactions.
