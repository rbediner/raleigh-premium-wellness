Studio Development Manager

Studio Development Manager

Help launch Raleigh’s next premium wellness destination.

We’re looking for a Studio Development Manager to help bring a new premium wellness concept to life in Raleigh and build the community around it from the ground up. This is a highly visible leadership role for someone who knows how to create buzz, build relationships, generate momentum, and help turn something new into something real. You’ll play a real role in shaping a wellness destination people feel excited to discover, trust, and make part of their lives.

Who This Could Be For

You may be a strong fit if you are well connected in your community, naturally social, credible, and energized by helping something new gain real momentum. You might come from fitness, wellness, hospitality, events, or another people-driven environment where you have learned how to create memorable experiences, build loyalty, and bring people together. You may also be earlier in your career but ready for a more visible role where your energy, instincts, and execution can help build something special from the ground up.

Why This Is Exciting

For the right person, this is a chance to help bring something real to life from the ground up. You would work closely with ownership, help shape early momentum, and play a visible role in building a wellness destination people in Raleigh come to know, trust, and talk about. It can feel like more than a job. It can feel like a meaningful next chapter with room to grow as the business expands.

What You Might Find Yourself Doing

This is not an all-inclusive list, but here are some of the ways you may make an impact in this role:

Community Presence

  • Creating community moments people remember, including local give-back events, appreciation events, and other initiatives that help the studio show up in meaningful ways
  • Hosting launch events, open houses, networking nights, pop-ups, and wellness gatherings that introduce the studio to the market
  • Helping create educational and experiential moments that make the brand approachable, memorable, and worth talking about

Partnership and Growth

  • Building smart relationships with gyms, fitness studios, apartment communities, members’ clubs, and other local partners
  • Turning local visibility into momentum through follow-up, introductions, invitation-based events, and partner-led activity
  • Helping shape signature local experiences that make the brand feel social, shareable, and exciting

Launch Leadership

  • Working closely with ownership on launch planning, community presence, early growth, and overall market momentum
  • Helping recruit, energize, and support the team across technicians and front-of-house roles
  • Coordinating the moving pieces that help the studio open strong, including setup, vendor coordination, inventory readiness, training support, and pre-opening organization
