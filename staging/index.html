Studio Development Manager

Studio Development Manager

Help launch Raleigh’s next premium wellness destination.

We’re looking for a Studio Development Manager to help bring a new premium wellness concept to life in Raleigh and build the community around it from the ground up. This is a highly visible leadership role for someone who knows how to create buzz, build relationships, generate momentum, and help turn something new into something real. You’ll play a real role in shaping a wellness destination people feel excited to discover, trust, and make part of their lives.

Who this could be for

You may be a great fit if...

You are deeply connected in your local community, naturally social, highly credible, and the kind of person others know, trust, and listen to. You love bringing people together, creating energy, and helping something new gain real traction. You may be ready for a more meaningful next chapter and excited by the idea of putting your relationships, instincts, and presence to work in building something visible and special.

You might come from fitness, wellness, hospitality, events, or another people-driven environment where you have learned how to connect with people, create memorable experiences, and build loyalty. You may feel ready for a bigger leadership role where your presence, people skills, and commercial instincts can have a greater impact.

You might be early in your career, but already know you’re ready for more. You are polished, ambitious, and hungry for the kind of opportunity that gives you real visibility, real responsibility, and a real chance to help build something from the ground up. You want to be close to the action, part of something early, and in a role where your energy, instincts, and execution can genuinely move the business forward.

What matters most is that you are community-minded, commercially aware, highly personable, and energized by helping build momentum around something new. You care about health, wellness, and longevity, and you feel excited by the idea of helping bring a wellness destination to life that people genuinely want to discover, trust, and make part of their lives.

What makes this meaningful

Why this is exciting

For the right person, this is the kind of opportunity that can change your trajectory.

You would not simply be stepping into something already built. You would be helping bring something to life from the ground up, from buildout and launch to early momentum, community presence, and the team that grows around it.

You would have the chance to shape something real, lead people, and play a visible role in creating the kind of wellness destination that becomes known in the Raleigh market. That is not nothing. For the right person, there is real pride in being able to say: I helped launch this.

This also has the potential to feel like more than a job. It can feel like a lifestyle, a community, and a meaningful next chapter. You would be building something alongside a small, close-knit team, working closely with ownership, and helping create the kind of energy, culture, and presence that people want to be part of.

In a world that feels increasingly digital and impersonal, there is something meaningful about building a role around human connection, trust, community, and real presence. Those qualities are central here, and they are only becoming more valuable.

For the right person, this could become more than a launch role. As the business grows, there may be opportunities for broader leadership scope tied to future expansion.

How you may make an impact

What you might find yourself doing

This is not an all-inclusive list, but here are some of the ways you may find yourself making an impact in this role:

  • creating community-building moments that genuinely make a difference, such as charity drives, teacher appreciation events, and other local give-back initiatives that help the studio show up in ways people remember
  • organizing and hosting events that introduce the studio to the market and bring people into the community around it, such as studio open houses, local business networking nights, pop-up events, wellness workshops, happy hours, Self-Care Sunday series, seasonal wellness events, back-to-school self-care days, and community walk or run sponsorships
  • helping create educational and experiential moments that make the brand feel approachable, memorable, and worth talking about, such as post-workout recovery lounges, lymphatic health talks, hydration and recovery pop-ups, and wellness-focused socials
  • identifying smart co-branded opportunities with gyms, fitness studios, apartment communities, members’ clubs, and other local partners that can expand awareness, strengthen credibility, and drive referrals
  • turning local visibility into real momentum through follow-up, relationship building, personal introductions, invitation-based events, and partner-led moments that keep people engaged and connected to what is coming
  • helping shape signature local experiences, such as members-only open houses, Sculpt & Sip socials, yoga and lymphatic recovery sessions, and outdoor wellness day activations that make the brand feel shareable, social, and exciting
  • working closely with ownership on launch planning, community presence, early growth, and the studio’s overall momentum in the market
  • helping recruit, energize, and support the team across technicians and front-of-house roles
  • coordinating the moving pieces that help the studio open strong, including setup, vendor coordination, inventory readiness, training support, and pre-opening organization, while also helping lead the day-to-day business once the studio opens
