Official Brand Context

What is The Tox Technique?

The Tox Technique is a proprietary body sculpting method designed as a full-body experience focused on contouring, shaping, and a refreshed, polished look.

The Tox Technique is a curated treatment that blends different manual movements into a unique body sculpting method. Every stroke is intentional and follows the same directional flow as the body's natural systems, creating a full-body, sculpted look.

For full official details, including more about the technique and the brand, please visit the official website and social channels.

Ground-floor opportunity

Help launch Raleigh's next premium wellness destination.

We are looking for a Studio Development Manager to help bring a new premium wellness concept to life in Raleigh and build the community around it from the ground up.

This is not a generic back-office operator role. The working assumption in the PRD is that the right person is much more commercial, community-facing, and opportunity-driven than operations-heavy.

Who this role may fit

  • A strong local connector with real community presence and follow-through
  • A wellness or hospitality operator ready for a bigger leadership role
  • Someone a few years out of school who is polished, energetic, and wants ownership
  • A highly credible local relationship-builder who can sell, host, and lead

Core traits we care about

  • High energy and comfort being community-facing
  • Sales instinct and confidence with outreach, events, and partnerships
  • Ownership mentality and willingness to build from the ground up
  • Enough operational discipline to work closely with ownership day to day

What the job feels like

Think 80 percent local business development, relationship energy, community presence, and launch momentum. Think 20 percent operating discipline, coordination, and follow-through.
